# Sources and what still needs checking

Written after a deliberate verification pass. The point of this file is that whoever
picks this up does not have to re-derive which claims are solid.

## Tiers

**Tier 1, cite directly.** Government primary sources. Ministry and agency websites,
official government portals, press releases from the body itself, constitutions and
statutes, and the annual budget or supply documents. Also: an international
organisation's own publication when the claim is about that organisation's index.

**Tier 2, reputable but verify the specific number.** Established regional and national
press. Numbers drift, and a figure quoted in a 2023 article may have been superseded.

**Tier 3, do not publish without primary confirmation.** Consultancy and company-setup
blogs, press-release redistribution sites, niche trade outlets carrying a claim that
appears nowhere else, and anything summarising a document rather than quoting it.

## Verified against primary sources

- The UAE industrial target, the baseline figure and the world ranking goal, from the
  ministry's own strategy page and launch release.
- The UAE ministry's formation date and the three bodies merged into it, from the
  ministry's own about page.
- The UAE product offtake figures including the achieved value and the sector
  breakdown, from the ministry's own offtake page, which carries an update date.
- The UAE minister's dual role and the chairmanship of the development bank, from the
  ministry's own interview release.
- The UAE industrial council's composition, chair, deputy chair, rapporteur, seat count
  and first-meeting agenda, from the ministry's news releases.
- Abu Dhabi's own industrial strategy figures, investment, jobs and export targets, and
  its launch by the Crown Prince as Executive Council chairman, from the Abu Dhabi
  government and the federal government portal.
- The UAE constitutional provisions on emirate ownership of natural resources and on
  residual powers sitting with the emirates.
- That industrial licences are issued by each emirate's economic department, or by a free
  zone authority inside a free zone.
- The custodian of the UAE long-term national vision, from the cabinet affairs ministry.
- Singapore's sector roadmap count, economy coverage and the fact that they are
  co-created by government, industry and unions, from the trade ministry and an official
  annex.
- Singapore's economic council creation date, chair and cluster structure, from an
  official press release.
- Singapore's 2026 strategy review: five committees, co-chairs, consultation scale,
  recommendation count and thrust count.
- Singapore's permanent foresight unit and where it sits, from its own site.
- Singapore's land agency share of industrial land, its skills framework scope and role
  count, and its career conversion salary support level, from agency sites.
- That a union officer sits on the boards of both the investment board and the
  enterprise agency, from a trade ministry board-changes release.
- Singapore's research consortium membership scale and joint lab outcomes, from the
  research agency and the investment board.
- The quantum access agreements, from the counterparty's own investor relations page and
  a press release.

## Corrected during verification

- The UNIDO rank trajectory. Earlier drafts stated a single figure. It is 35th, then
  29th in the 2023 report, then 27th.
- The chipmaker stake. Softened from a precise percentage to "controlling shareholder",
  since ownership has shifted and a government has since taken a small equity position.
- The apex of the UAE system. "Seven rulers" is constitutionally correct and
  operationally misleading; the council of seven ratifies rather than initiates. Named
  by office instead: President and Prime Minister.

## Dropped as unverifiable

- A claim that a UAE sovereign fund co-funds a named foreign university's quantum
  programme toward a specific qubit count. Single source, could not confirm.
- A named "sovereign superconducting hardware initiative". One niche outlet only. The
  substance survives in better-sourced form: the research institute develops its own
  superconducting quantum chips alongside partner platforms.

## Unverified, currently kept out of the artifact

- **The UAE local content mechanics.** The bid-evaluation weighting and the certificate
  validity period came from service-provider pages, not the ministry. Get these from the
  ministry's own programme guidelines before publishing either number.
- **Singapore's talent passes.** Work passes for global specialists exist, but the
  current names, tiers and eligibility have not been checked. The Singapore capability
  option list marks this.
- **The launch month of the UAE strategy.** The ministry's own release and secondary
  reporting give different dates in 2021. The artifact states 2021 only and the apex node
  says the month is unsettled. Resolve before putting a date on a slide.
- **US tariff status after the 150-day statute.** Reimposed measures were due to expire
  absent congressional extension. This post-dates the research and must be checked before
  the US map is built.

## A note on currency

Two of the four countries are mid-transition. Singapore's strategy review reported in
May 2026 and a national council has already been created from it. The US legal basis for
tariffs changed in February 2026 and may have changed again. Anything in those two maps
should be re-checked before client use; the Brazil and UAE material is more stable.
